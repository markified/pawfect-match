## 1. Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Open the Pawfect Match app**
2. **Tap "Register"** on the login screen
3. **Fill in your information:**
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
4. **Tap "Register"** to create your account
5. **You're in!** You'll be automatically logged in

### Logging In

1. Enter your **email** and **password**
2. Tap **"Login"**
3. Access your dashboard

### First Steps After Registration

✅ **Complete your profile** - Add your photo and location  
✅ **Add your first dog** - Register your dog's profile  
✅ **Browse matches** - Start exploring potential breeding partners  
✅ **Join the community** - Introduce yourself and your dog

## 2. User Profile Setup

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Tap the **Profile** tab (bottom right)
2. See your profile information, statistics, and menu options

### Editing Your Profile

1. Go to **Profile** tab
2. Tap **"Edit Profile"**
3. Update your information:
   - **Profile Photo:** Tap the camera icon to upload
   - **Name:** Your full name
   - **Phone Number:** Contact number (optional)
   - **Location:** City/State for local matches
4. Tap **"Save"** to update your profile

### Your Statistics

Your profile displays:
- **Total Dogs:** Number of dogs you've registered
- **Completed Matches:** Successful breeding arrangements
- **Average Rating:** Your reputation score (1-5 stars)

### Getting Verified

To earn the **Verified Breeder** badge:
- Complete 5+ matches
- Maintain 4.0+ average rating
- Be active for 3+ months
- Have a complete profile

**Benefits of Verification:**
- Build trust with other breeders
- Higher visibility in search results
- Priority match suggestions
- Exclusive features (coming soon)

## 3. Adding Your Dogs

### Registering Your First Dog

1. Go to **My Dogs** tab
2. Tap the **+ (Add)** button
3. Fill in your dog's information:

#### Required Information
- **Photos:** Upload 1-5 high-quality photos
- **Name:** Your dog's name
- **Breed:** Select from dropdown list
- **Age:** Enter age in months
- **Sex:** Male or Female
- **Size:** Small, Medium, Large, or Giant

#### Additional Details
- **Color:** Primary coat color
- **Temperament:** Select traits (Friendly, Playful, Calm, etc.)
- **Health Information:** Medical history, vaccinations, genetic testing
- **Availability:** Toggle ON if available for breeding

4. Tap **"Save"** to add your dog

### Photo Tips

✨ **Best Practices:**
- Use clear, well-lit photos
- Show your dog from multiple angles
- Include full body shots
- Capture your dog's personality
- Avoid blurry or dark images

### Editing Dog Information

1. Go to **My Dogs** tab
2. Tap the dog you want to edit
3. Tap **"Edit"** button
4. Update any information
5. Tap **"Save"**

### Deleting a Dog Profile

1. View the dog's detail page
2. Tap **"Delete"** button
3. Confirm deletion
4. **Note:** This action cannot be undone

### Managing Availability

Toggle your dog's availability:
- **ON (Available):** Dog appears in match searches
- **OFF (Unavailable):** Dog hidden from matches but still visible on your profile

## 4. Finding Matches

### Browse Available Dogs

1. Go to **Home** tab
2. Scroll through available dogs
3. View **compatibility percentage** on each card

### Understanding Compatibility Scores

**Our Smart Matching Algorithm:**
- **🧬 35% Breed Match** - Same breed is ideal
- **📅 25% Age Compatibility** - Optimal age difference
- **⚤ 15% Sex Compatibility** - Opposite sex required
- **❤️ 25% Temperament** - Shared personality traits

**Score Ranges:**
- **90-100%:** Excellent match
- **75-89%:** Great match
- **60-74%:** Good match
- **45-59%:** Fair match
- **Below 45%:** Consider carefully

### Viewing Dog Details

1. Tap any dog card
2. View complete profile:
   - **Photo Gallery:** Swipe through all photos
   - **Basic Information:** Breed, age, size, color
   - **Temperament:** Personality traits
   - **Health Info:** Medical details
   - **Owner Profile:** View breeder information
   - **Reviews:** Read owner's reputation

### Owner Information

Each dog profile shows:
- Owner's name
- Location
- Verification status
- Tap to view full profile and reviews

## 5. Managing Match Requests

### Sending a Match Request

1. View a dog you're interested in
2. Tap **"Send Request"** button
3. **Select your dog** from the dropdown
4. **Write a message** to the owner:
   - Introduce yourself
   - Explain why you're interested
   - Share your breeding goals
   - Be respectful and professional
5. Tap **"Send"**
6. Wait for the owner to respond

### Writing Great Request Messages

✅ **Do:**
- Introduce yourself and your dog
- Explain your breeding goals
- Highlight compatibility factors
- Be polite and professional
- Include contact information

❌ **Don't:**
- Send generic copy-paste messages
- Be pushy or demanding
- Omit important information
- Use inappropriate language

### Viewing Your Requests

**Match Tab** shows two categories:

#### Received Requests (Sent to You)
- Requests from other breeders
- Review each request carefully
- Accept or decline

#### Sent Requests (You Sent)
- Track your outgoing requests
- See status: Pending, Accepted, or Declined
- Cancel pending requests if needed

### Responding to Requests

**When you receive a request:**

1. **Review the Request**
   - Read the breeder's message
   - Check their dog's profile
   - View compatibility score
   - Review their reputation

2. **Accept or Decline**
   - **Accept:** Start coordinating the breeding
   - **Decline:** Politely decline (optional message)

3. **After Accepting**
   - Exchange contact information
   - Discuss breeding terms
   - Arrange meeting (if needed)
   - Coordinate logistics

### Managing Active Matches

**Accepted Requests:**
- Access request details anytime
- View both dogs' profiles
- See contact information
- Mark as completed when done

### Completing a Match

**After successful breeding:**

1. Open the match request
2. Tap **"Mark as Completed"**
3. Confirm completion
4. **Both parties can mark as completed**
5. Enables review submission

## 7. Reviews & Reputation

### Why Reviews Matter

Reviews help:
- Build trust in the community
- Showcase your reputation
- Guide breeding decisions
- Earn verified status

### Viewing Reviews

**On Dog Profiles:**
- Scroll to "Reviews" section
- See recent reviews of the owner
- View star ratings and comments

**On Your Profile:**
- View all reviews you've received
- Check your average rating
- Read feedback from partners

### Submitting a Review

**After Completing a Match:**

1. Go to **Profile** → **"Completed Matches"**
2. Find the match you want to review
3. Tap **"Leave Review"**
4. Rate your experience:
   - **Select 1-5 stars**
   - **1 star:** Very dissatisfied
   - **2 stars:** Dissatisfied
   - **3 stars:** Neutral
   - **4 stars:** Satisfied
   - **5 stars:** Very satisfied
5. Write your review (20-500 characters):
   - Describe your experience
   - Mention communication quality
   - Note professionalism
   - Discuss dog health/genetics
   - Share overall impression
6. Tap **"Submit Review"**

### Writing Helpful Reviews

✅ **Good Review Example:**
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "Excellent experience with Sarah! Her Golden Retriever is beautiful and healthy. She was very professional, communicated clearly, and provided all health records. The breeding went smoothly, and we're excited about the upcoming litter. Highly recommend!"

❌ **Unhelpful Review Example:**
> ⭐⭐⭐ "It was okay."

### Review Guidelines

**Be Specific:**
- Mention concrete details
- Describe what went well
- Note any issues professionally

**Be Fair:**
- Consider the full experience
- Don't let minor issues overshadow positives
- Rate objectively

**Be Constructive:**
- If rating low, explain why
- Offer suggestions for improvement
- Focus on facts, not emotions

### Building Your Reputation

**Ways to Improve Your Rating:**
- Complete matches successfully
- Communicate promptly and clearly
- Be honest about your dogs
- Follow through on commitments
- Provide health documentation
- Be professional and courteous

## 9. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### "I can't log in"
- Check email and password spelling
- Ensure caps lock is off
- Reset password if forgotten
- Check internet connection

#### "My photos won't upload"
- Check image file size (max 5MB)
- Ensure stable internet
- Try different photo
- Close and reopen app

#### "I don't see any matches"
- Ensure your dog is marked available
- Check your location is set
- Browse different breeds
- Be patient - more users joining daily

#### "My request was declined"
- Don't take it personally
- Try other matches
- Improve your profile
- Write better request messages

#### "Compatibility score seems wrong"
- Algorithm weighs multiple factors
- Not just breed compatibility
- Consider age, temperament, sex
- Lower score doesn't mean incompatible

## 10. Safety Guidelines

### Meeting in Person

**When arranging to meet:**

✅ **Do:**
- Meet in public place first
- Bring a friend if possible
- Share location with someone
- Meet during daylight
- Trust your instincts

❌ **Don't:**
- Share personal address immediately
- Meet alone in isolated areas
- Rush the process
- Ignore red flags

### Protecting Your Information

**Share Wisely:**
- Use app messaging initially
- Wait to share phone number
- Don't share financial info
- Verify user legitimacy first

**Red Flags:**
- Requests for money
- Pressure to decide quickly
- Reluctance to meet in person
- Vague or inconsistent information
- Too good to be true offers

### Breeding Safety

**Health First:**
- Verify all health records
- Require genetic testing results
- Check vaccination status
- Consult your veterinarian
- Don't compromise on health

**Legal Protection:**
- Use written contracts
- Agree on terms beforehand
- Document everything
- Understand local laws
- Consider legal consultation

**Financial Safety:**
- Discuss costs upfront
- Put agreements in writing
- Use secure payment methods
- Get receipts
- Be wary of unusual requests

### Protecting Your Dogs

**Health Screening:**
- Both dogs fully vaccinated
- Recent vet checkup
- Disease testing complete
- Parasite-free

**Supervised Breeding:**
- Professional supervision recommended
- Safe environment
- Emergency vet contact ready
- Monitor both dogs

**Post-Breeding Care:**
- Follow-up vet visits
- Monitor health
- Document pregnancy
- Share updates with partner

### If Something Goes Wrong

**If You Feel Unsafe:**
- End communication immediately
- Block the user
- Report to app administrators
- Contact local authorities if needed

**If Breeding Issues Arise:**
- Consult veterinarian immediately
- Document all issues
- Communicate with partner
- Review contract terms
- Seek legal advice if necessary

**If Dispute Occurs:**
- Try resolving directly first
- Review written agreements
- Keep all communication records
- Seek mediation if needed
- Consider legal options as last resort
